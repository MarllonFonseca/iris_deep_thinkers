# Frases da voz (ElevenLabs)

Uma voz, as mesmas definições para todas. Guardar cada uma como MP3 com o nome indicado, nesta pasta.
Crédito: «Voice generated with ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io)».

## 1. Instructions before each test

- `intro_acuity.mp3`: Near acuity test. Keep the screen at 40 centimetres, a little further than your usual reading distance. The camera warns you if you are too close or too far. We start with the left eye: cover your right eye with your palm, without pressing. A line of letters appears. For each letter, left to right, tap the same letter on the keypad, or say it out loud with the microphone on. The score shows how many letters you got right. When the line is complete, tap Next or say “next”. Do not bring the screen closer to see better. If you cannot read, tap “Can’t read”. Then repeat with the other eye. When you are ready, tap start, or say “next”.
- `intro_amsler.mp3`: Amsler grid. Cover your left eye with your palm, without pressing. We start with the right eye. Hold the screen 33 centimetres from your face, about the length of a bent forearm. Look only at the black dot in the centre. Without looking away, notice any bent or wavy lines, blurred, dark or missing areas, or squares of different size. Tap the squares where you saw something different. If you saw nothing, tap “All looks straight”, or say “all straight”. Then cover your right eye and repeat. When you are ready, tap start, or say “next”.
